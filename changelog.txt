Empire Overview - changelog

0.1.5-alpha (not released yet)
- Trends: the mod records the empire every game month, page open or not
  (data\Logs\DW2Overview\history, one file per game). Loading an earlier
  save drops the months after it.
- Resources: a Trend line on every row and card (stock over the last 5
  years, with the level to keep), and a Trends chart beside the supply table:
  up to 8 resources as % of the level to keep (so any two compare), stock,
  net rate or price, over 2 years, 10 years or the whole game. Add one from
  its details (Show in Trends); its chip turns the line on and off, and rows
  and cards in the chart carry a mark in its colour.
- Under the supply table, a lane for each resource that was low or critical,
  month by month; hover for how long, click to open it.
- A resource's details: stock, rate (and how long the stock lasts) and
  prices as cards, and its stock over the whole game. Rows open the details
  too, as cards do.
- The Rows / Cards switch sits next to the list it changes (Resources,
  Construction, Ships).

0.1.4-alpha (2026-11-27)
- Fix: the page answered "Not found" for everyone but me. The release carried
  a development setting that served the page from a folder on my machine. The
  mod now always serves its own page, and the log says which folder it used.

0.1.3-alpha (2026-11-26)
- Abandoned ships and bases (the game's Exploration list): Needs attention says
  who is on each ("Lucky Beggar repairing", "nobody on it, dangerous"), and the
  Ships tab has an Abandoned group with health and danger.
- Toasts: the resources of a suggested site keep their spacing.

0.1.2-alpha (2026-11-26)
- Advisor suggestions show the site's resources, as the game's dialog does (a
  suggested mining station: what is there and how much).
- Resource pills follow the game's marks: the name in red for a production
  shortage, in yellow for oversupply (the tooltip says how much per second).
  One pill everywhere (cargo, places, suggestions, palette), with the type as
  the left stripe; a click on any pill opens that resource.
- Command palette: a place shows its resources and who is mining it;
  Shift+Enter opens it on the page (Enter still goes there in the game).
- Resources: the search also matches places ("as693" lists what is there);
  Mined and New locations have the type filter; tab counts follow the filters;
  the resource details show places in three columns.
- Location cards keep the place's name readable with two badges; the message
  log button shows its key (Q).

0.1.1-alpha (2026-11-26)
- Ctrl+Alt+9 in the game opens the page in your default browser; the game shows
  the shortcut once when a save loads. (The game's own shortcuts ignore Ctrl and
  Alt and use every letter, so the shortcut uses a number.)
- Mining ships count as mining. They mine without a station, so a resource mined
  only by ships showed "Not mined": now its next step says "Mined by ship at
  <place> (<ship>)", new mining locations show "<ship> mining", the "Mined"
  filter includes it, and Needs attention no longer says "nobody on it".
- Square preview image.

0.1.0-alpha (2026-11-26)
- First public alpha: Overview, Construction, Ships, Colonies, Resources,
  Research and Messages; alerts and Watch rules; command palette; game controls;
  the port in the launcher's settings.
